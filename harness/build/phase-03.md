# Phase 03: City and vehicles

## Purpose

City and vehicles for the standalone Bengali game.

## Scope

4–5 connected locations; traffic; rickshaw/CNG/auto/bicycle; entry/exit, driving physics/camera; checkpoints; day/evening/night/rain.

## Non-goals

Admin, multiplayer and referral.

## Dependencies

Explicit approval after Phase 02.

## Acceptance criteria

Connected playable city and vehicles meet performance and culture requirements.

## Verification plan

Driving/collision tests, streamed world/mobile performance, weather/audio visual checks.

## Approval gate

Do not start without explicit user approval.
