# Asset and software provenance

## Cricket armor player

- `public/assets/models/dhaka-character-hero.glb` is an editable, rigged interpretation of the user supplied front and back cricket character reference. The green and red armor, Bengali jersey lettering, fictional crest and all costume pieces are authored in `assets/source/cricket-hero.mjs`. The shaped head uses the MakeHuman CC0 source credited below. Run `node assets/source/generate.mjs --hero` to regenerate it. Bengali lettering is rasterized into the GLB and regeneration requires a Bengali font such as Nirmala UI or Noto Sans Bengali.
- The 17-joint bind pose and nine animation payloads match the shipped humanoid. The model is the built-in appearance of the solo player when the original appearance is selected in the catalog. Explicit catalog appearance selections and uploaded character models continue to apply.

## Phase 03 vehicles and weather

- The bicycle and battery auto-rickshaw are original editable models in `apps/game/src/vehicles.ts`. The rideable rickshaw and green CNG reuse the authored Phase 01 art, with independently rotating wheels and boarding anchors.
- `public/assets/models/ride-animations.glb` contains the original `DriverSteer` and `RiderPedal` clips for the existing rig. Regenerate the 28,824-byte bank with `node assets/source/generate-mission-animations.mjs --phase03`. The source lives in `assets/source/generate-mission-animations.mjs`.
- Bengali checkpoint signs and the rain-streak texture are generated from editable project code. Night lighting, wet asphalt and vehicle cues reuse the existing materials and shipped audio catalog.

## Phase 02 action art

- The palm tap, inflatable baton swing, bullhorn gesture and paper popper gesture are original skeletal clips for the existing 17-joint character. The 56,192-byte `public/assets/models/action-animations.glb` contains those four clips. The editable source is `assets/source/generate-mission-animations.mjs`. Rebuild the bank with `node assets/source/generate-mission-animations.mjs --phase02`.
- `apps/game/src/combatProps.ts` contains the original vinyl baton, bullhorn, festival popper and curled paper effects. The game reuses its existing sound catalog. Newspaper photographs are captured from the rendered game.

## Phase 01 character and street refinement

- `public/assets/models/dhaka-character-phase01.glb` adds three sculpted face variants with vertex color, a shaped hairline, original eye/braid geometry, improved sleeve drape and a shared skin micro-normal. It retains the original 17-joint bind pose and all nine animation payloads. The original `dhaka-character.glb` and the accepted B copy remain unchanged. Regenerate with `npm run assets:phase01`.
- Face geometry derives from the MakeHuman [CC0 base mesh](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj), pinned to commit `a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482`, SHA-256 `8E761E6624B8F54536409135D1636DA63B32486A90D4897F84E121D144F6FB4C`. The local source and license are in `assets/source/phase01-character-art/makehuman/`; the game includes only the cropped and adapted head surface. No MakeHuman application code, full source body, real-person likeness or photo texture is shipped. Editable extraction/sculpt and rig source: `phase01-face.mjs` and `generate.mjs`.
- Roof tanks with moulded ribs, lids and supply pipes, and check-weave gamchas draped over balcony railings are original scene assets in `world.ts`. The user photos remain references for local visual details.
- Meshy returned a free-plan creation restriction for both planned model requests; no Meshy asset was added. The same verified account had 100 credits before and after.

## Audio expansion A

`public/assets/audio` contains 65 original Foley/music WAVs generated by `assets/source/generate-audio.mjs`: eight footstep surfaces with three variants, action/vehicle/ambient cues and five original music compositions. No commercial songs or third-party sound samples were used. The root audio license covers that synthesized bank, not the separately licensed speech subdirectory.

`public/assets/audio/voices` contains 23 fictional Bengali speech clips synthesized offline with Piper 1.8.0 and the [bn_BD google medium model](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/bn/bn_BD/google/medium). Its model card lists OpenSLR37 CC BY-SA 4.0 and a CMU permissive notice; both are retained in `public/assets/licenses/Bengali-Piper-model-card.txt`. Speech adaptations are distributed separately under CC BY-SA 4.0 with source notices in `public/assets/audio/voices/LICENSE.txt`. Exact original fictional lines, anonymous speaker IDs and model SHA-256 are in `assets/source/generated/voices-provenance.json`. No politician, party recording or named-person imitation is used. Source scripts: `generate-voices.mjs`, `synthesize-voices.py`.

Piper is an offline [GPL-3.0 authoring tool](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl), isolated in ignored `.tools/piper-python`; it is not bundled into the browser or server runtime. The older 2023 CLI was incompatible with the current model's multi-codepoint phoneme map; it was replaced by the current Python API without changing the model. `.tools` executables/models are not runtime download requirements.

`public/assets/models/foot-contacts.json` is derived from the actual original GLB sole geometry and authored leg-chain forward kinematics by `assets/source/analyze-footsteps.mjs`. Footsteps are triggered by animation phase crossings, not a generic interval.

## Original project artwork

- `public/assets/models/dhaka-character.glb`: original 17-joint skinned character with nine clips (Idle, Walk, Run, Throw, Hit, Pickup, EnterVehicle, Seated, ExitVehicle). Revision 2 refines face/limb anatomy, weighted cotton folds/collar, optional panjabi/kameez/dupatta, hair and moustache, plus pelvis stance compensation. Milestone B adds original 1.4-second boarding, 2.6-second seated breathing and 1.25-second exit clips; accepted A geometry, rig and the original six animation payloads remain byte-identical. `tests/boarding-assets.test.ts` checks the accepted archive, actual sampler durations, normalized skeletal rotations and enter/seated/exit continuity. These structural checks do not establish visual fit inside the CNG; runtime observation is still required. Editable source: `assets/source/generate.mjs`; regenerate with `npm run assets`. No scanned person or copied identity.
- Environment and vehicle geometry is authored in `world.ts`, `vehicles.ts`, `sceneDetails.ts`, `characterStyle.ts`, `art.ts` and `game.ts`.
- Built-in imagegen produced `assets/source/generated/dhaka-surface-atlas-v2.png` using the supplied street/rickshaw/CNG pictures as visual references. Exact prompt and roles: `assets/source/generated/prompts.md`. Runtime materials in `public/assets/textures` contain 1024² albedo and 512² approximate normal/ORM maps; regenerate conversions with `npm run assets:materials`. These are estimated/generated materials, not measured photogrammetry.
- All runtime textures, HDR lighting, fonts and models are bundled locally; no remote/CDN runtime dependency. Dust, foliage, cotton weave, Bengali signs and fictional posters are original procedural artwork.
- Traffic bed, motor hum, horns and feedback tones: original offline synthesis in `assets/source/generate-audio.mjs`, played through the Web Audio graph in `audio.ts`. Speech sources are documented separately above.
- UI vector icons and map: original SVG in `main.tsx`.

Original project artwork is supplied as part of this project for the user's use and editing. Source is procedural, not a Blender `.blend` file; Blender was unavailable locally. Characters share a base skeleton/anatomy with garment, hair, skin-tone and scale variations. This is not photo-to-character technology or cloth simulation.

The user's eleven original photographs remain unchanged at `D:/game image`; source copies, dimensions, hashes and roles are archived under `assets/source/references`. Their ownership/licenses were not independently established. No complete supplied photograph, real political portrait, real organisation logo or real-person face texture is published in the game.

## Milestone C original additions

Four outer Dhaka sectors, road connectors, textile/print shops, hand-painted Bengali signs and posters, utility wires, speech props, the stage access ramp and fictional office details are editable original TypeScript models in apps/game/src/sectors.ts and sectorBlueprints.ts. They reuse the existing licensed plaster/asphalt/enamel maps and original rickshaw/CNG/bus geometry. No external 3D generation or credits were used. The original four-argument CNG silhouette is preserved; optional moving-wheel modelling retains three real wheel hierarchies for the two scripted traffic loops.

The fictional স্বস্তি সেবা ambulance, interior/stretcher, moving doors/lights/wheels, medical identifiers, rig-linked phones and folded Bengali paper props are original editable models in ambulance.ts/ambulanceGeometry.ts and missionProps.ts. They do not reproduce a real hospital, party or relief-organization logo. Characters and paramedics instantiate the preserved original17-joint base rig, not portraits.

assets/source/generate-mission-animations.mjs authors twelve same-rig skeletal clips into public/assets/models/mission-animations.glb (165984bytes), preserving the952896byte baseGLB and its nine clips. assets/source/analyze-mission-footsteps.mjs derives Carry/AssistedWalk foot contacts from the actual GLB skeletal samples and weighted sandal sole vertices. mission-foot-contacts.json records both GLB hashes, durations and sole minima; the game merges these contacts into A's animation-driven footstep system. Reproduce with npm run assets:missions. Browser observation is still required for visual motion, blending and audio timing approval.
